Home Dashboard

Home Dashboard is a private, non-commercial display for one household. It runs on a Raspberry Pi on the family's own home network and shows the family's calendar, to-do list, the weather and a photo slideshow on a wall screen.

It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts. The only person who signs in is the household's own Google account owner, to let the screen read their calendar and tasks.
