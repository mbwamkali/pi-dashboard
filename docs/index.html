Home Dashboard

Home Dashboard is a private, non-commercial wall display for family households. Each household runs its own copy on a Raspberry Pi on its own home network, showing that family's calendar, to-do list, the weather and a photo slideshow.

It is shared with family members only. There is no website to sign up for and no user accounts: each household signs in once with its own Google account, on its own device, to let its screen read its calendar and tasks.
